About GCalc

Fast, free, and privacy-first calculators built for everyone. No sign-up. No limits. No tracking.

Our Mission

We believe that everyone deserves access to powerful, professional-grade calculation tools without barriers. GCalc was built to remove friction from everyday math — whether you are calculating a mortgage, tracking your health, or solving a complex formula.

Our goal is simple: make the best calculators on the internet, keep them free forever, and respect your privacy while doing it.

Our Story

GCalc started as a single-page project to solve a simple problem — most online calculators are cluttered with pop-ups, slow to load, or force you to download an app. We wanted something better.

What began as one clean calculator grew into a collection of thirteen specialized tools, each designed with the same principles: speed, clarity, and zero bloat. Every calculator works instantly in your browser, with no accounts, no data collection, and no paywalls.

What We Value

🔒 Privacy First

All calculations happen locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or track any of your data.

🚀 Speed

Instant results as you type. No server round-trips, no loading spinners, no waiting.

📱 Universal Design

Works beautifully on phones, tablets, and desktops. One experience, every device.

🎯 Zero Bloat

No downloads, no accounts, no email spam. Just the tool you need, when you need it.
