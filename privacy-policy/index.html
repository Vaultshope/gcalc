Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GCalc ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use gcalc.app and the calculators offered on it. The short version: all calculations happen locally in your browser, and we do not collect, store, or share your personal data.

By using GCalc, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect personal information. Every calculator on GCalc — BMI, mortgage, loan, calorie, and the rest — runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter (height, weight, loan amount, interest rate, etc.) are processed on your device and are never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details (unless you email us directly)
  • Calculator inputs or results
  • Account credentials (there are no accounts)
  • Payment information (everything is free)

3. Cookies

GCalc currently does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or identification. The site sets no first-party cookies and reads no third-party cookies.

If this changes in the future (for example, to remember your preferred units), this policy will be updated and, where required by law, a consent notice will be shown.

4. Analytics

We do not currently run analytics on GCalc. If we enable analytics in the future (such as Google Analytics 4), it will be configured to collect only aggregated, non-identifying usage data — for example, page views and approximate region — and this policy will be updated before that data collection begins.

5. Advertising

GCalc is free to use. To keep it that way, we may display advertising in the future. If we do:

  • Ad partners (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads
  • You may be able to opt out of personalized advertising through your ad settings (for example, Google Ads Settings)
  • This policy will be updated with full details before any ads go live

Advertisements will always be clearly labeled and will never be mixed into calculator results.

6. Affiliate Links

GCalc may participate in affiliate programs in the future. If a page contains an affiliate link, it will be disclosed on that page. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate relationships never influence calculator results. Calculations are pure math and are the same for everyone.

7. Third-Party Services

GCalc relies on a small number of third-party services to function:

  • Google Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google's servers. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and standard request data. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Hosting: The site is served by a static hosting provider, which processes standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and delivery purposes.

No other third-party scripts, trackers, or pixels are used.

8. Children's Privacy

GCalc is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), in line with COPPA and GDPR requirements. Since we do not collect personal data from anyone, no data about children is collected, used, or shared.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under regulations such as the GDPR (EU/EEA) or CCPA (California), including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because GCalc does not collect or store personal data, there is generally no data for us to access, correct, or delete.

If you have questions about your rights, or if you emailed us and want your correspondence deleted, contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Data Security

GCalc is served over HTTPS, encrypting traffic between your browser and the site. Because calculations run locally and no personal data is collected or stored on our side, the risk of a data breach involving your information is minimal by design.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when advertising or analytics are introduced. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version. Material changes will be summarized on this page.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at hello@gcalc.app.
